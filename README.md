# Peripatos

> Learn while you walk. Learn while you work.

In ancient Athens, Aristotle's students learned by walking alongside him through the *peripatos*, the covered walkway of the Lyceum. Knowledge came from movement and conversation, not from sitting still.

**Peripatos** brings that idea to your workday. It reads your daily notes, detects the concepts you run into on the job, and turns them into a personal, living **syllabus**: explanations, hands-on coding labs, reading lists and spaced reviews, all built around what you actually do.

Your work is the curriculum.
